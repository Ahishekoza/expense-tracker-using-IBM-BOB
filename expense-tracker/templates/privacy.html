Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

Spendly ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our personal expense tracking application.

2. Data We Collect

We collect minimal personal and financial information necessary to provide you with an effective expense tracking experience:

Account Information: When you register, we collect your name, email address, and hashed login credentials.
Financial and Expense Data: Details of expenses you log, including amounts, categories, descriptions, dates, and payment methods.
Usage Data: Technical information such as browser type, device information, and interaction logs to improve platform performance.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to power the Spendly service:

• To manage your account and authenticate your access.
• To calculate, categorize, and visualize your spending insights and summaries.
• To troubleshoot bugs, maintain system security, and enhance usability.
• We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal or financial data to advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Storage and Security

We employ industry-standard security practices to protect your data. All communication is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS, passwords are cryptographically hashed, and database access is strictly restricted. While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

5. Third-Party Services

We do not share your confidential financial records with third parties except as required to provide core infrastructure (such as secure cloud hosting and database providers). Any third-party vendors we work with are bound by strict data processing and confidentiality agreements.

6. Your Rights and Data Retention

You retain complete control over your information. You may view, update, export, or delete your logged transactions and profile information at any time from your account settings. Upon account deletion, all associated financial records are permanently removed from our active databases.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements to our service or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions or inquiries regarding your privacy or data protection practices, please contact us at privacy@spendly.app.
